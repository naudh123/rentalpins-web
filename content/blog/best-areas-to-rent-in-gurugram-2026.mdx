Gurugram is not one rental market. A builder floor in Sushant Lok, a high-rise flat on Golf Course Road and a PG bed in Sector 14 serve very different renters at very different rents. The fastest way to choose is to start from your office and compare a few neighbouring areas on the map.

This guide covers the six Gurugram areas with the most live listings on RentalPins and who each one suits.

## Sushant Lok: closest to Cyber City

[Sushant Lok](/rentals/in/gurugram/sushant-lok) Phases 1 to 3 have builder floors and low-rise apartments close to MG Road metro, Galleria Market and Cyber City. It is the most popular choice for professionals who want a short commute without paying Golf Course Road rents. PG and rooms are also common here.

**Good for:** Cyber City and MG Road office workers, couples, sharers.

## Golf Course Road: high-rise societies on the Rapid Metro

[Golf Course Road](/rentals/in/gurugram/golf-course-road) has large societies with clubhouses, pools and security along the Rapid Metro. Rents are among the highest in the city, but so are amenities. It also has office space for companies wanting a prominent address.

**Good for:** families, senior professionals, people who want society amenities.

## Sohna Road: more space for the money

[Sohna Road](/rentals/in/gurugram/sohna-road) and Sectors 47 to 49 offer gated societies with schools and markets nearby, often at rents below Golf Course Road for similar flats. Traffic builds up at office hours, so check your commute before signing.

**Good for:** families who want space and amenities at moderate rents.

## Palam Vihar: independent floors near Delhi

[Palam Vihar](/rentals/in/gurugram/palam-vihar) is an established colony of independent floors near the Delhi border, with quick road access to Dwarka, the airport and Dwarka Expressway.

**Good for:** families wanting an independent floor, people who travel often to Delhi or the airport.

## Sector 14 and old Gurugram: budget rooms and PG

[Sector 14](/rentals/in/gurugram/sector-14-gurugram) and the older sectors around Sector 14 Market have rooms, PG and floors at lower rents than the newer parts of Gurugram, close to Huda City Centre metro.

**Good for:** students, budget renters, shop owners.

## Sector 56: Golf Course Extension on a budget

[Sector 56](/rentals/in/gurugram/sector-56-gurugram) has society flats near the Sector 55–56 Rapid Metro station, a short ride from Golf Course Road.

**Good for:** professionals on Golf Course Road and the southern sectors.

## Builder floor or society flat?

Builder floors usually give more space and fewer neighbours, but ask who handles water, parking, lift and power backup. Society flats include maintenance, security and amenities; ask whether the quoted rent includes maintenance. Compare both types in the same area before deciding.
