Indore is Madhya Pradesh's largest commercial city. Students come for its colleges and coaching institutes, professionals work along AB Road and the Super Corridor, and families live across the planned schemes. Each group rents in a different part of the city, so start from where you work or study.

This guide covers the Indore areas renters search most on RentalPins and who each one suits.

## Vijay Nagar: the commercial heart

[Vijay Nagar](/rentals/in/indore/vijay-nagar-indore) is Indore's busiest commercial area on AB Road, with flats in Scheme 54, Scheme 78 and nearby schemes, PG for students and young professionals, and office space. Malls, restaurants and the BRTS corridor are on the doorstep. Rents are higher than in some other areas, so compare nearby schemes too.

**Good for:** professionals, startups and clinics looking for offices, young sharers.

## Palasia: central and established

[Palasia](/rentals/in/indore/palasia) is central Indore, with flats in the residential lanes of New Palasia and Old Palasia and busy shops around 56 Dukan. It is close to Geeta Bhawan, Saket Nagar and AB Road. The quieter lanes suit families better than the main roads.

**Good for:** families, shop owners, people who want to be near markets and hospitals.

## Bhawarkuan: student Indore

Bhawarkuan has rooms and PG near coaching institutes and DAVV colleges. Ask about meals, sharing, study space and the notice period, and start searching a few weeks before your batch begins.

**Good for:** students and coaching aspirants.

## Super Corridor: newer flats near the IT parks

The Super Corridor connects the airport side with the IT parks, and Indore Metro is being built along it. It has newer apartments for people working at the IT parks, though shops and services are still developing in some pockets.

**Good for:** IT park employees who want a newer flat.

## Rau: budget on the southern edge

Rau has budget flats and rooms on the southern edge of the city, close to IIM Indore. The commute to the centre is longer, but the rents are lower.

**Good for:** budget renters, people working in south Indore.

## Flat or independent house?

Planned schemes have both flats and independent houses. Houses give more space, but confirm water supply, parking and power backup. Also ask whether maintenance is included in the rent.
