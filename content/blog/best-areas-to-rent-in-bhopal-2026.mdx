Bhopal is a calm, green city with an easy-going rental market. Government employees, students at MANIT and the engineering colleges, AIIMS staff and families all rent here, mostly in New Bhopal's planned colonies and along the southern corridors. Choose your area by distance to your office or college, then compare rents.

This guide covers the Bhopal areas renters search most and who each one suits.

## MP Nagar: the commercial centre

MP Nagar is Bhopal's commercial centre, with offices, coaching institutes, shops and flats nearby. It has the largest choice of office and shop listings in the city.

**Good for:** office workers, businesses, coaching students.

## Arera Colony and Shahpura: green family colonies

Arera Colony and Shahpura are green, planned colonies with family flats and independent houses close to schools, hospitals and markets. They are among the most sought-after addresses in New Bhopal.

**Good for:** families, government employees.

## Kolar Road: newer flats at moderate rents

Kolar Road in south Bhopal has newer flats and townships at moderate rents. The commute to MP Nagar is longer, but you get a newer home for the money.

**Good for:** families and professionals who want a newer flat.

## Indrapuri and Ayodhya Bypass: student rooms

Indrapuri and Ayodhya Bypass have rooms and PG near engineering colleges. Ask about meals, sharing, study space and the notice period, and start searching a few weeks before the session begins.

**Good for:** engineering students, young professionals on a budget.

## Hoshangabad Road: the southern corridor

Hoshangabad Road has flats and townships along the city's southern corridor, close to Rani Kamalapati station and with good road links. It also has a growing number of shops and offices.

**Good for:** families, AIIMS and southern-corridor employees.

## Near the lakes

Areas around the Upper Lake and VIP Road are popular for their views and greenery, with rents varying a lot by locality. Compare pins carefully before visiting.

## What rent to expect

From live RentalPins listings in November 2026, the middle half of 2 BHK asking rents in Bhopal was about **₹10,500 – ₹22,000 a month**. Rooms, PG and houses vary by area, so compare live pins near your office or college.

Bhopal owners usually ask for one to two months' rent as deposit. Agree the amount, electricity billing and maintenance in writing before moving in.
