Ghaziabad is one of the most affordable places to rent in NCR, and most renters here work in Delhi or Noida. The right area is the one with the easiest commute to your office: by metro, NH-24 or the Namo Bharat line. Start from your workplace and compare a few areas on the map.

This guide covers the Ghaziabad areas renters search most and who each one suits.

## Indirapuram: between East Delhi and Noida

Indirapuram has society flats and builder floors with quick road access to East Delhi and Noida. Markets, malls and schools are close by, which makes it one of Ghaziabad's most popular rental areas.

**Good for:** professionals working in Delhi or Noida, families.

## Vaishali and Kaushambi: on the Blue Line

Vaishali and Kaushambi are on the Blue Line metro next to Anand Vihar, a major rail and bus hub. They have flats and rooms, and the Delhi commute here is the easiest in Ghaziabad.

**Good for:** Delhi metro commuters, frequent train and bus travellers.

## Vasundhara: family flats in the middle

Vasundhara sits between Indirapuram and Vaishali, with family flats and builder floors near parks, schools and markets.

**Good for:** families who want space close to both Indirapuram and the metro.

## Raj Nagar Extension: newer societies for less

Raj Nagar Extension has many newer gated societies at rents usually below the areas next to Delhi. You get more space for the money, but the commute is longer.

**Good for:** families who want a larger flat in a newer society.

## Crossings Republik: township living on NH-24

Crossings Republik is a large township along NH-24 with flats close to Noida and to colleges on the highway. Rooms and PG for students are common here.

**Good for:** Noida commuters, students at NH-24 colleges.

## Society flat or builder floor?

Society flats include security, maintenance and often power backup, but confirm whether maintenance is in the rent and whether the society has rules or move-in charges for tenants. Builder floors may give more space; ask about parking, water supply and who maintains the building.

## What rent to expect

From live RentalPins listings in November 2026 (middle half of asking rents within 25 km):

- 1 BHK flat: ₹7,000 – ₹16,500 a month
- 2 BHK flat: ₹18,000 – ₹30,000 a month
- 3 BHK flat: ₹19,000 – ₹26,000 a month, mostly in newer societies further from Delhi
- Shop / office: ₹28,500 – ₹49,000 a month

Ghaziabad owners usually ask for one to two months' rent as deposit, and many societies require tenant police verification.
