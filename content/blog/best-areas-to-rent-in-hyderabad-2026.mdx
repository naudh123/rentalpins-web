Hyderabad's rental market follows its IT corridor. Most renters work in HITEC City, Gachibowli or the Financial District, and the right area depends far more on that commute than on the rent. Start from your office on the map and compare two or three neighbouring areas.

This guide covers the Hyderabad areas renters search most on RentalPins and who each one suits.

## Kondapur: between HITEC City and Gachibowli

[Kondapur](/rentals/in/hyderabad/kondapur) sits between HITEC City and Gachibowli, with apartments, gated communities and independent houses. Rents are often a little lower than in Madhapur for similar flats, and PG with meals are common for new joiners.

**Good for:** IT professionals at HITEC City or Gachibowli, sharers, new joiners.

## Madhapur and HITEC City: walk to work

[Madhapur](/rentals/in/hyderabad/madhapur) is at the heart of HITEC City, with PG, studio flats and apartments within walking distance of many offices. It is served by the HITEC City and Durgam Cheruvu metro stations. Rents are higher than Kondapur, but you save the most time on traffic.

**Good for:** new joiners, people who want to walk to the office, startups looking for office space.

## Gachibowli and the Financial District: gated communities

Gachibowli and Nanakramguda have large gated communities with clubhouses and power backup, close to the Financial District, the University of Hyderabad and IIIT Hyderabad. Manikonda, Narsingi and Kokapet nearby offer newer flats, often at lower rents, though shops are still developing in some pockets.

**Good for:** families, Financial District employees, students and faculty at the Gachibowli campuses.

## Kukatpally and Miyapur: metro on the Red Line

Kukatpally and Miyapur are on the Red Line, with a direct ride to Ameerpet and a change to the Blue Line for HITEC City. Flats here usually cost less than in the IT corridor, which makes them popular with people who prefer a predictable metro commute.

**Good for:** budget-conscious families, people who commute by metro.

## Secunderabad and Ameerpet: central and well connected

Secunderabad is a major railway hub and Ameerpet is the main metro interchange. Both have flats, rooms and PG for people who work in the central city or travel often.

**Good for:** central-city office workers, students at Ameerpet training institutes, frequent train travellers.

## Gated community or independent house?

Gated communities include security, maintenance and amenities, but ask whether maintenance is in the rent and whether there is a move-in fee. Independent houses often give more space for the same money; confirm water supply, parking and who maintains the common areas.

## What rent to expect

From live RentalPins listings in November 2026 (middle half of asking rents within 25 km):

- PG / room: ₹5,500 – ₹16,000 a month
- 1 BHK flat: ₹8,000 – ₹22,000 a month
- 2 BHK flat: ₹13,000 – ₹26,500 a month
- 3 BHK flat: ₹26,500 – ₹41,500 a month

Hyderabad owners commonly ask for two to three months' rent as deposit. Agree the amount and refund timeline in writing.
