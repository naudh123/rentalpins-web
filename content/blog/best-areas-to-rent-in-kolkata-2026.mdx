Kolkata is one of India's more affordable big-city rental markets, but the choice of area still matters. Getting from South Kolkata to Sector V at evening rush hour can take much longer than the map suggests. Start from where you work or study and compare a few nearby areas on the map.

This guide covers the Kolkata areas renters search most on RentalPins and who each one suits.

## Salt Lake: closest to Sector V

[Salt Lake (Bidhannagar)](/rentals/in/kolkata/salt-lake) has flats and independent houses in planned blocks close to Sector V and City Centre. The East–West Metro now links Sector V with Sealdah and Howrah, and PG for new joiners are common.

**Good for:** Sector V employees, families who want established markets, new joiners.

## New Town: newer complexes near the IT hubs

New Town has newer apartment complexes with wide roads, parks, Eco Park and shopping, close to the New Town IT hubs. The large supply keeps rents competitive, so compare several complexes before deciding. Ask about maintenance, power backup and parking, which usually follow the complex rules.

**Good for:** IT professionals, families who want a newer flat with amenities.

## Garia and Jadavpur: student rooms near the Blue Line

Garia and Jadavpur have rooms, PG and budget flats near Jadavpur University, South Kolkata colleges and Blue Line metro stations. Single rooms in family houses are common here.

**Good for:** students, young professionals on a budget.

## Park Street and Ballygunge: central and premium

Park Street and Ballygunge have central flats and premium apartments close to offices, markets and hospitals. Expect higher rents than the suburbs and a much shorter commute to central offices.

**Good for:** central-city office workers, families wanting a central address.

## Behala and Tollygunge: family homes in the south

Behala and Tollygunge have family flats and independent houses in south and south-west Kolkata, close to schools and hospitals. Tollygunge is on the Blue Line.

**Good for:** families, people working in South Kolkata.

## Flat in a complex or older house?

Older neighbourhoods often have independent houses and floors with more space. Newer complexes in New Town and parts of Salt Lake offer security and power backup. In either case, ask whether rent includes maintenance and how electricity is billed.

## What rent to expect

From live RentalPins listings in November 2026 (middle half of asking rents within 25 km):

- PG / room: ₹3,000 – ₹12,000 a month
- 2 BHK flat: ₹8,500 – ₹15,000 a month
- 3 BHK flat: from about ₹14,000 a month

Kolkata owners usually ask for one to three months' rent as deposit. Agree the amount and refund terms in writing before you pay.
