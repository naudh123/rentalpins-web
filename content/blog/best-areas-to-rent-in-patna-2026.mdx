Patna's rental market is shaped by students. Thousands come every year for competitive exam coaching and college, alongside families in the city's planned colonies. For students, being able to walk to the coaching centre matters most. For families, the deciding factors are a quiet lane, schools and water supply.

This guide covers the Patna areas renters search most on RentalPins and who each one suits.

## Boring Road: walk to coaching

[Boring Road](/rentals/in/patna/boring-road) is Patna's busy coaching and shopping street. The side lanes have rooms and PG within walking distance of many coaching centres. The main road is noisy; SK Puri and Boring Canal Road nearby have flats in quieter lanes.

**Good for:** coaching students, shop owners.

## Kankarbagh: rooms and flats at scale

[Kankarbagh](/rentals/in/patna/kankarbagh) is one of Patna's largest residential areas, with many rooms, PG and flats close to coaching centres in Rajendra Nagar and the Ashok Rajpath area. Some lanes get waterlogged in heavy rain, so ask the owner about this before a monsoon move.

**Good for:** students, families, students sharing a flat.

## Rajendra Nagar: coaching and the station

Rajendra Nagar has student rooms near coaching centres and family flats close to Patna Junction.

**Good for:** students, families who travel by train often.

## Bailey Road: west Patna's main corridor

[Bailey Road](/rentals/in/patna/bailey-road) runs across west Patna towards Danapur, with flats, rooms and offices along the road and in side lanes. Shastri Nagar and Raja Bazar nearby have markets, schools and hospitals.

**Good for:** families and professionals working in west Patna offices and hospitals, clinics and businesses.

## Patliputra Colony: quiet and planned

[Patliputra Colony](/rentals/in/patna/patliputra) is a quieter planned area with independent houses and flats, close to Boring Road and Pataliputra Junction. Rooms in family houses suit professionals and students who want calm.

**Good for:** families wanting space, working professionals.

## Room, PG or flat?

A PG includes meals and suits students who want to focus on study. A single room in a family house is often cheaper but comes with house rules. Sharing a flat with friends gives you a kitchen and more space. In every case, ask about meals, electricity backup, water supply, curfew and the notice period.
