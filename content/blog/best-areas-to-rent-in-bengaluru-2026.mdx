In Bengaluru, your commute matters more than almost anything else. Outer Ring Road, Silk Board and the tech-park roads can turn a short distance into a long drive, so the best area to rent is usually the one closest to your office. Start there, then compare rent and home type.

This guide covers the five Bengaluru areas with the most live listings on RentalPins.

## BTM Layout: PG capital of south Bengaluru

[BTM Layout](/rentals/in/bengaluru/btm-layout) has one of the largest choices of PG, shared rooms and 1 BHK flats in the city, between Silk Board, Madiwala and Koramangala. Many new joiners start here because there is so much choice within walking distance.

**Good for:** new joiners, students, budget renters working on Outer Ring Road or Hosur Road.

## Koramangala: the startup hub

[Koramangala](/rentals/in/bengaluru/koramangala) has flats, shared apartments and PG across Blocks 1 to 8, close to startup offices, cafés and the Forum area. Rents are higher than in BTM, but the commute to many offices is short.

**Good for:** young professionals, sharers, people who want a lively neighbourhood.

## HSR Layout: planned sectors and family homes

[HSR Layout](/rentals/in/bengaluru/hsr-layout) has apartments, independent floors and PG in planned sectors with parks, close to Outer Ring Road and Koramangala.

**Good for:** couples and families working on Outer Ring Road or Sarjapur Road.

## Whitefield: for ITPL and the eastern tech parks

[Whitefield](/rentals/in/bengaluru/whitefield) is east Bengaluru's IT hub, with gated apartments and PG around ITPL, Hoodi and Brookefield. The Purple Line metro links it to the city centre.

**Good for:** people working at ITPL and in the eastern tech parks.

## Hebbal: for Manyata Tech Park and the airport

[Hebbal](/rentals/in/bengaluru/hebbal) suits people working at Manyata Tech Park or travelling often to the airport, with apartments around Hebbal Lake, Nagawara and RT Nagar.

**Good for:** Manyata Tech Park employees, frequent flyers.

## PG, shared flat or 1 BHK?

PG usually includes meals, cleaning and Wi-Fi, which suits new joiners. Shared flats give more privacy and a kitchen for groups of colleagues. A 1 BHK suits couples and people who want their own space. Compare all three in the same area on the map before deciding.

## What rent to expect

From live RentalPins listings in November 2026 (middle half of asking rents within 25 km):

- PG / room: ₹6,500 – ₹16,500 a month
- 1 BHK flat: ₹11,000 – ₹17,000 a month
- 2 BHK flat: ₹18,500 – ₹26,000 a month
- 3 BHK flat: ₹28,500 – ₹49,000 a month

Bengaluru deposits are often higher than in other cities, so agree the amount and refund terms before paying.
